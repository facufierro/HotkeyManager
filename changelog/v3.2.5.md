# Hotkey Manager v3.2.5

## Changes

- Fixed the Copilot-key → Right Ctrl remap registering the key as pressed many times (and sometimes leaving Right Ctrl stuck down, which silently turned other hotkeys into Ctrl+ combos) when the key was held: Right Ctrl is now pressed once and always released when the key is let go.
- Fixed hotkeys going dead or laggy after the app had been idle in the tray: the app and its hotkey process are now kept out of Windows Efficiency Mode throttling, and the hotkey hooks are reinstalled immediately when the app detects it was throttled or asleep, so hotkeys respond right away instead of after a delay.
